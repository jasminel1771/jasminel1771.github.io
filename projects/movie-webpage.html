Movie Webpage Project

Context

At the end of our learning of basic HTML and CSS, my class was instructed to create a webpage for a trailer of a movie we made up, using our knowledge of the basic HTML and CSS codes we have learned, as well as our creativity. This project involved collaborative partnership, meaning that everyone had to work and collaborate with a partner to create their webpage. Every partnership had the freedom to pick their own storyline, style, and genre. However, everyone needed the have the same requirements to demonstrate our understanding of what we have learned. My teacher also offered extra credit if you added a sequel. My partner and I decided to base our movie off a game called Undertale, so our movie title is simply UNDERTALE: The Movie.

Content

The movie my partner and I created for this project is based on the game Undertale, so our storyline is closely related to the game's storyline. The main character is a character named Frisk and the movie is about an adventure that he goes on and he meets monsters along the way. You can read a little more about the movie in the link provided towards the bottom of the page to view the preview. There was a set of requirements that needed to be included in our project in order to meet the criteria and get full credit. The first requirement was to create a plan using Markdown language before we started working on the actual project with HTML and CSS. This helped with making the working process smoother and more organized. For the HTML component, we had to include heading(s), paragraph(s), div(s) and span(s), Google Fonts that weren't used in class yet, and an input and submit component. For the CSS component, we needed to have selectors (elements, class, and id) and the box model (padding, margin, and border). We also needed to have at least 2 images that are local. For extra credit, we also added a sequel to our project at the end.

Challenges

Before working on the project, we had to come up with what genre our movie was going to be and what the storyline was roughly going to be. This was the first challenge my partner and I encountered. We tried to come up with ideas but nothing really came to mind. We first thought of several genres, such as action, comedy, thriller, or fantasy, but nothing stood out and we still had no idea what the storyline would be. But then, my partner pitched in the idea of using a game that he played as a reference, which was Undertale. I did not know anything about this game, but since I had no ideas of my own, I decided to give it a try. After researching about the game and it's story, I actually found it to be interesting and the storyline was good for a movie. So, with the help with my partner in confirming information, we came up with the storyline for UNDERTALE: The Movie.

For the rest of the project, me personally, I felt that we completed the project with ease and no major challenges or problems. One minor challenge I faced was changing the size of an image. I wanted to make the image smaller, but when I tried, the image still remained the same size. After I tried a few variations of code to see if I did something wrong, I still couldn't figure out what I did wrong and looked in my notes multiple times, so I asked my partner whether he knew how to make the image smaller. He was a little unsure as well and tried as well, but couldn't get it to work either. Instead of giving up, we searched online and got the code to work. We used the resources we had and I found W3Schools to be helpful source when I didn't know how to code something.

Takeaways

A major takeaway I got from this project is the importance of communication and collaboration. Throughout this project, my partner and I tried our best to communicate with each other and express what ideas we had. Without communicating, our project may not have turned out the way my partner and I would like it to be or something we are satisfied with. Collaboration was also a big part during this project. My partner and I split our responsibilities evenly so that we could finish on time and one person didn't have to do all the work. We had to work together to finish all our parts because if one person did not finish their part, the project would be incomplete and missing things. When collaborating, communication should naturally follow, so while working on this project, me and my partner talked and came up with ideas and solutions together instead of just letting the other person take all the lead. Me and my partner finished our project rather early, and I believe it's because we talked and had good collaboration that allowed us to finish earlier than others who did not communicate or collaborate as much. This showed me the importance of working together and talking with one another.

Next Steps

In the future if I had more time, I would like to add a little bit more to the storyline and possibly add my own little twist and creativity into the storyline. My top priority was to meet the project criteria and finish what was needed for full credit, so I spent less time focusing on the visual aspect. I would continue to add onto the visual and aesthetics of the webpage if I had more time. In the future, I hope to create webpages that will be more complex and interesting.
